Welcome home. This is Engine OS.

Your desktop and folders are real and saved on the installed disk.
Files starts in Home. Open Documents, Desktop, Downloads or Pictures
from the sidebar. Click a folder to open it. The location shows where you are.

The upper < and > buttons go Back and Forward. Up opens the parent folder.
The lower < and > buttons page through a long list.
Choose New folder, type your own name and press Enter to save it.
Cancel or Escape keeps everything unchanged. Delete asks for confirmation;
a folder must be empty before it can be deleted.

System drive shows the installed native volume. System folders are
protected from changes through Files. Your own folders are under Home.
Other disks and partitions are not automatically mounted in this release.

Drag a window by its title bar. Resize its lower-right corner.
The underscore minimizes it; its taskbar button restores it. x closes it.
Appearance opens Settings. Save preferences keeps your accent and window style.
My files and Documents on the desktop open additional Files windows.

You do not need a network, terminal or Calculator for these tasks.
For a later disk boot: power off, disconnect only the CD/DVD ISO,
keep the SATA hard disk connected, then start the VM again.
The ISO's Open installed desktop can resume a matching installation.

The product is Engine OS. Original DOS and Windows applications come later.
