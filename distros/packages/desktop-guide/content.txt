Welcome to Engine OS.

Open Files, Text Editor, Settings, Calculator or Downloads from the icons or Start.
Move a window by dragging its title bar. Resize its bottom-right corner.
Use the underscore to minimize; click its taskbar button to restore it.

Text Editor saves with Save or Ctrl+S. Save as lets you choose a new filename.
Reopen it through Files. Files asks before deleting an item.
Unsaved changes are protected by a close confirmation. The current text limit is 8191 bytes.
Settings changes the accent and light/dark appearance. Save preferences keeps your choices.
Calculator is optional. Removing it preserves the desktop and your documents.
An offline package cache lets you reinstall it from Settings.

Downloads uses real HTTPS. Check that your virtual Ethernet cable is connected
and your virtual hardware clock is correct UTC. Use Ctrl+L to replace the address,
then Download or Enter. Open Downloads shows completed files.
Cancelled or failed transfers do not add a partial file. Certificate errors cannot be bypassed.
The current download limit is 64 MiB; the supported network family is Intel 8254x/82574.

This is original native Engine OS software. DOS and Windows apps are the next milestone.
Advanced details remain optional; ordinary desktop tasks need no terminal commands.
The online handbook and exact supported VM limits are at https://engineos.brixcanvas.com.

Advanced Terminal is available from Start for optional commands and verbose details.
