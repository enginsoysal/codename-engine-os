## Qualified limits

M71-VM-v1 names x86-64 QEMU BIOS/UEFI optical/raw USB and VMware Workstation 26.0.1 BIOS/UEFI, SATA/AHCI 512-byte sectors, 128-MiB/two-CPU/20-GiB VMware targets and Secure Boot off. Disk management requires 1024 × 768. OS installation remains 2 GiB to 2 TiB and replaces the whole selected disk; no selected-partition/dual-boot installation.

GPT editing supports canonical 128 × 128-byte entries; primary MBR has four slots. Extended/logical mutation, resize/move, non-destructive conversion, NTFS/ext formatting, secure erase and universal/physical power-loss guarantees are not implemented. FAT32 requires 64 MiB; aligned Engine data formatting requires 1 MiB. Extra data partitions are not automatically mounted in the desktop. Failed/torn edits may need explicit recovery rather than rollback.

Retained [M7 bounds](m7-v0.7.0.md) apply: eight windows, 8,191-byte Editor, 1,024 native entries, 64-MiB download, TLS 1.2 with correct UTC/RDRAND, e1000e MSI/NAT in VMware, trusted supervisor services and narrow CPL3 native apps. This is no general app scheduler, authenticated multi-user OS, GPU-accelerated desktop or original DOS/Windows runtime. Those tracks remain M8; physical hardware/factory Secure Boot remain unqualified.
