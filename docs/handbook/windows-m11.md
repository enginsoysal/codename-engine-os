# Windows and appearance in M11

M11.4 is qualified within its named window/appearance VM scope.
[Download and install](install-m114.md) · [release limits](../releases/m114-v0.11.4.md).
Expanded setup and full final regression follow as M11.5 and M11.6.

## Arrange your workspace

- Drag a title bar to move its window. Some of it may leave the screen, while
  enough remains reachable to bring it back.
- Double-click the title bar, or click its middle window button, to maximize.
  Repeat to restore the previous size and position.
- Drag any edge or corner to resize. Apps keep a usable minimum size.
- Drag to the left or right screen edge to preview a half-screen placement;
  release to keep it. Drag to the top to maximize. Escape cancels a drag.
- Press Windows+Left/Right to snap, Windows+Up to maximize and Windows+Down to
  restore (or minimize an already normal window). A narrow screen may not fit
  two copies of a large app; use Maximize instead.
- Hold Alt and press Tab to see open windows. Press Tab again to advance, or
  Shift+Tab to reverse. Release Alt to choose. Escape keeps your current window.
- Right-click a title bar or running taskbar window for its window commands.
  Alt+Space opens the same menu. Move/Size use arrows, Enter to keep and Escape
  to cancel. Alt+F4 closes through the ordinary unsaved-work review.

## Make it yours

Open **Settings** or the desktop **Appearance** launcher. Preview an accent,
Light/Dark windows, wallpaper and display size. Choose **Save preferences** to
keep these and normal app window sizes for your user. Start/taskbar customization
remains separate and does not delete applications or folders.

Auto adapts to the actual display; 4K normally uses 200%. Larger text needs enough
screen space. If a size cannot fit the workspace, choose a smaller size or Auto.
The interface explains a refused size; it does not silently overlap controls.
These appearance choices affect the shell, not foreign Windows applications.
